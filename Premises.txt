In manufacturing plants and refineries, storage tanks often contain hazardous liquids like oils, chemicals, or fuels.
Manual inspection is risky and inefficient.

In this system:

An ultrasonic sensor continuously measures the distance from the tank top to the liquid surface.

Data is sent via MQTT from edge devices.

Kafka acts as a fault‑tolerant buffer and streaming backbone.

SQL Server stores historical and live data.

Power BI provides real‑time visualization and trend analysis.

This allows operations teams to:

Monitor tank levels in real time

Detect abnormal draining or filling

Avoid overflows and shortages

Reduce human exposure to hazardous environments

That’s enterprise‑grade wording.
